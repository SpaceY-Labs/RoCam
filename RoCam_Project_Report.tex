\section*{Project Overview}

RoCam is a deployable, autonomous camera tracking system that locks onto and follows high-speed model rockets in real time. Model rockets exceed \textbf{Mach\,3}, crossing a telephoto frame in under one second---making manual tracking physically impossible. Commercial tracking rigs cost over \textbf{\$10{,}000}, placing them out of reach for student teams and hobbyists. RoCam delivers stable \metric{1080p}{@ 60\,fps} video of critical flight events (staging, parachute deployment) for under \metric{\$500}{CAD}---a \textbf{20$\times$ cost reduction} versus commercial alternatives.

% ══════════════════════════════════════════════
\section*{Primary Use Case}

The McMaster Rocketry Team and hobbyist rocket clubs need autonomous video capture of launches for post-flight analysis. An operator arms the system via a web dashboard; RoCam autonomously detects, tracks, and records the rocket from liftoff through recovery. The operator monitors the live feed, overrides gimbal controls, manages recordings, and downloads stabilized video---all from a laptop browser.

\section*{Extended Use Cases}

RoCam's core technology---real-time small-object detection, sub-millisecond gimbal control, and edge AI inference---constitutes a \textbf{general-purpose high-speed tracking platform} with applications far beyond rocketry:

\vspace{2pt}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}XX@{}}

\domainbox{shield-alt}{Defense \& Security}{%
Detect and track fast, small airborne objects for \textbf{counter-UAS surveillance}, perimeter monitoring, and missile-test observation. Low cost and portability enable rapid deployment at forward operating bases. The modular architecture supports radar cueing and threat-classification model integration.}
&
\domainbox{futbol}{Sports Broadcasting \& Analytics}{%
Autonomously follow a soccer ball, baseball, hockey puck, or tennis serve at broadcast quality \textbf{without a dedicated camera operator}. Sub-millisecond detection and 60\,Hz gimbal control keep fast objects centered---ideal for highlight generation, player analytics, and amateur event coverage.}
\\[6pt]

\domainbox{rocket}{Aerospace \& Launch Services}{%
Scaled with longer-range optics and larger gimbals, RoCam can serve as a \textbf{low-cost optical tracking station} for sounding rockets, satellite launches, and re-entry vehicles---tasks that typically require million-dollar cinema-grade mounts.}
&
\domainbox{car-side}{Motorsport \& High-Speed Testing}{%
Track vehicles at \textbf{200+\,km/h} on test tracks and race circuits for crash analysis, aerodynamic study, and automated race footage. The same predictive gimbal loop and small-object pipeline apply directly.}
\\[6pt]

\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{%
\domainbox{dove}{Wildlife \& Environmental Monitoring}{%
Bird migration tracking, bat colony surveys, and fast-moving wildlife observation share the challenge of locking onto small, erratic targets against open sky---a problem RoCam's vision pipeline is purpose-built to solve.}}

\end{tabularx}

\section*{System Architecture}

\vspace{-2pt}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}XX@{}}
\textbf{\color{accent}Computer Vision} \newline
Custom YOLO26s-P2 on NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano via TensorRT FP16. Detects targets as small as 10--30\,px at \metric{60}{FPS} with $<$\,1\,ms latency (\metric{94.3\%}{precision}, \metric{89.8\%}{recall}). GPU-shader digital zoom keeps targets centered.
&
\textbf{\color{accent}Gimbal Control} \newline
Two-axis servo gimbal driven by STM32F405RG (Rust firmware), closed-loop proportional control at 60\,Hz over custom binary UART protocol with CRC8 checksums.
\\[4pt]
\textbf{\color{accent}Software Stack} \newline
Fault-tolerant multi-process backend (Python/Flask) with shared-memory IPC isolating CV, livestream, control, and transcode processes. React/TypeScript frontend for live video, telemetry, and recording management.
&
\textbf{\color{accent}ML Pipeline} \newline
15,800+ labeled images with 4,000 negative samples. Two-phase training (strong augmentation $\rightarrow$ fine-tuning) on NVIDIA H100, achieving \metric{mAP50-95}{= 0.7651} and \metric{mAP50}{= 0.9623}.
\end{tabularx}
