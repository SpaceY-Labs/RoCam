\speakernote{%
  [~1 min] Our deployed model is YOLO26s trained for 420 epochs
  with aggressive mosaic augmentation and 4,000 COCO hard negatives.
  In training on the H100, we achieve mAP 50-to-95 of 0.694.
  After ONNX export and TensorRT FP16 compilation on the Jetson
  Orin Nano, the deployed model achieves mAP 50-to-95 of 0.631 and
  mAP 50 of 0.875 --- meaning the model reliably detects rockets in
  real-time on edge hardware.  The 9 percent quantisation gap is
  expected for FP16 deployment.
